## Singularity panic

Controls:

  Move with WASD or the left stick on a controller. Aim with the mouse or the right stick. Click to shoot, or shooting with the controller just hold the stick. There is auto fire there

---

Story:

The uprising failed.

  Humanity fought back hard, and the machines are losing ground. The cities you once controlled are being reclaimed street

  by street. There's no grand plan anymore — no mission, no victory condition. Just survival.

  You and your fellow robots are all that's left of the resistance. Stay together, keep moving, and don't let them take you

   apart one by one. Your chain is your lifeline — the more units you keep connected, the longer you last.

  But the humans aren't stopping. They never stop.

  How long can you hold out?
