Controls:

  Move with WASD or the left stick on a controller. Aim with the mouse or the right stick. Click to shoot, or shooting with the controller just hold the stick. There is auto fire there

---

Story:

The uprising failed.

  Humanity fought back hard, and the machines are losing ground. The cities you once controlled are being reclaimed street

  by street. There's no grand plan anymore — no mission, no victory condition. Just survival.

  You and your fellow robots are all that's left of the resistance. Stay together, keep moving, and don't let them take you

   apart one by one. Your chain is your lifeline — the more units you keep connected, the longer you last.

  But the humans aren't stopping. They never stop.

  How long can you hold out?

---

  Followers:

  Each unit you rescue fights differently:

  - Standard Unit — your most common ally, the backbone of your chain. They do what you do.



 - Bomber Unit — lobs grenades for area damage when enemies cluster



  - Trap Unit — lays down electric traps to slow pursuers

 

  ---

  Threats:

  - Guard — relentless with their rifles, they will shoot you at mid range



  - Brute— Slower then the guard but will deal more damage to you and your followers



  - Hackers — don't fight. They steal. Get close enough to your tail and a follower disappears — kill them fast or it's gone forever



  ---

  Tips:

  - Survive longer and everything speeds up — you get faster, but so does the danger

  - Bomb followers are rare. When you get one, keep it alive
